# The Prophet ⚽

A friendly predictions app for the **2026 FIFA World Cup** — built for family and friends to compete, laugh, and celebrate together.

---

## What is this?

**The Prophet** is a social prediction game where you and your group predict the outcomes of World Cup 2026 matches. Everyone picks their scores, earns points for accuracy, and climbs the leaderboard. That's it. No money. No stakes. Just bragging rights.

> This app is not a betting platform and has no commercial purpose whatsoever. It exists purely for fun, shared moments, and friendly competition among people we care about.

---

## Features

- **Group play** — join a private group with an invite code and compete only with people you know
- **Match predictions** — pick your score for every World Cup match
- **Live leaderboard** — see who's leading the group in real time with a ranked scoreboard
- **Friend bonuses** — earn extra points for inviting friends to join your group
- **Simple login** — sign in with Google, email, or jump in as a guest

---

## Tech stack

- HTML5 + Tailwind CSS (Material Design 3 theme)
- Vanilla JavaScript
- Google Fonts (Inter) + Material Symbols icons
- Mobile-first responsive design

---

## Who is this for?

For the group chat that goes wild every time there's a big match. For the family reunion where someone always claims they "knew it." For friends who want a reason to keep watching even after their team is out.

No bets. No money. Just fun with people we appreciate.
